# Decision 0008: Minimum Lovable Product is the release goal

Date: 2026-10-07 (Asia/Bangkok).
Status: accepted from the user's explicit direction.

## Decision

The user said: "Forget the MVP concept, we gotta use Minimum Lovable Product,
not viable." Travel Life's first product target is therefore **Minimum Lovable
Product (MLP)**. A functioning build alone does not establish that it is ready.
The first experience must make the reconstructed journey feel useful and worth
keeping or sharing, while retaining the project's evidence and privacy standards.

This supersedes the *Minimum Viable Product* name and the assumption that a
merely working market-test loop is the product bar in decision 0002 and the
revision 6 planning artifacts. It does not itself select extra features, change
the accepted platform/source/share/maintenance decisions, or create a new
pre-launch interview or research gate.

## Planning consequence

Decision 0009 now defines the first loop and concrete acceptance signals: a
private lifetime map reveal, an owner-previewed replay video, and a deliberately
chosen lifetime-highlight share. Keep the scope deliberately small; use actual
product experience and rollout feedback to decide refinements. Existing files
and story IDs containing `mvp` remain historical filenames; their old
terminology is not the current product goal.
