- (direction) Owner proposed: core engine first, then a background AI engine strengthens results and solves hard cases quietly; an eval engine feeds corrections back so the AI self-improves.
- (direction) Owner agreed to three refinements: AI produces evidence not edits; quiet processing but visible changes; learning split into automatic personal rules and gated product versions. Asked to plan it and write detailed specs.
- (decision) Decision 0022 records the accepted direction. Roadmap epic M11, eight P1 stories, starts after M10.7 and reuses the M10.1 benchmark.
- (constraint) AI output is only a versioned clue record; it never writes trips, memberships or classifications. The M10 one-detector rules consume clues on rebuild.
- (constraint) Precedence: owner correction > personal rule > rules with clues > rules alone. Clues never override owner decisions or reviewed trips.
- (constraint) AI never removes a trip from Atlas by itself: a clue that would lower an Atlas trip creates a review card instead.
- (constraint) Promotion from Possible to Atlas by a clue class is off by default and enabled per class only after eval evidence and owner approval.
- (constraint) On-device models only for photo content (decision 0019); cloud metadata proposal path (src/ai, decision 0002) stays off and is out of scope.
- (constraint) Clues and personal rules stay private; clues are device-local and recomputable; personal rules are owner decisions that sync like corrections under decision 0015.
- (constraint) Background work respects platform scheduling (decision 0004), budgets and checkpoints; no promise of immediate updates.
- (constraint) Corrections are biased labels (missing trips are never corrected); the eval must include the synthetic benchmark, not corrections alone.
- (capability) CAP-1 Clue store: versioned clue records with subject, kind, value, provider version, independence key and validity, invalidated by evidence, privacy and provider changes.
- (capability) CAP-2 Rules consume clues: the one detector re-decides with clues and cites them in reasons; same input and clues give the same result.
- (capability) CAP-3 Background clue pass: runs after a rebuild in maintenance windows with budgets and checkpoints, Possible trips first, and shows a short updated note.
- (capability) CAP-4 Place clues from existing on-device sign reading, landmark matching, capture time offset and optional Gemini Nano; a name needs two independent agreeing clues.
- (capability) CAP-5 Photo-source clues: camera, imported (decision 0020), received, screenshot, download; non-own photos stop supporting trips.
- (capability) CAP-6 Same-trip clues: Possible trips or fragments that likely belong to one holiday become merge suggestions under decision 0017.
- (capability) CAP-7 Personal learning: corrections become personal rules shown in Settings and removable; effect on next rebuild.
- (capability) CAP-8 Eval engine and gate: benchmark with injected right and wrong clues, frozen hard cases, on-device keep/reject aggregates, versioned reports; new versions and promotions need no regression and owner approval.
- (decision) Visual-place proposals (visual_place_trip, visual_place_album) and travel-window review state become clue producers; their separate trip state is retired once clues reproduce their accepted results.
- (decision) Clue values are closed codes or offline gazetteer IDs, never free text from OCR or a model.
- (decision) Personal provenance rule asks once after the owner removes photos from the same source-folder category in three different trips.
