topic: Replay finalization truthfulness and fullscreen audio
updated: 2026-10-25T21:33
---

- (direction) Owner requests one detailed implementation plan/spec that consolidates partial Replay rendering, audio consistency and fullscreen speaker findings before further implementation.
- (decision) A first verified trip is an optional progressive preview, not a completed Replay; the UI must disclose remaining render and audio-mux work until the final artifact verifies.
- (capability) CAP-1 truthful finalization: owner can distinguish first-trip playback, finalizing, final-ready and failure without inferring a partial video is their final edited Replay.
- (capability) CAP-2 composition continuity: final replacement uses the one committed aggregate selection, included trip scope, direction and soundtrack; partial bytes cannot become the durable/share artifact.
- (capability) CAP-3 audio continuity: a custom/default soundtrack remains an acknowledged setting but final readiness requires the verified muxed AAC output; partial chapter playback states honestly disclose that soundtrack is not yet available.
- (capability) CAP-4 fullscreen listening: speaker mute/unmute stays available and preserves listening state in native fullscreen without rendering or changing the selected soundtrack.
- (constraint) Keep all owner media, source URIs, local audio paths and native errors private; use closed diagnostics and do not re-render or reselect media merely to enter fullscreen or change mute.
- (constraint) Preserve the approved lean self-serve Replay and trip-first editor: Update Replay remains one aggregate action, technical work stays automatic, and known work shows an updating percentage rather than premature completion.
- (constraint) Host checks cannot establish actual audio, fullscreen control reachability or visual truthfulness; Android and iOS owner-operated evidence is required.
- (non-goal) Do not add a multitrack editor, source-video audio, cloud audio upload, background audio catalog, automatic publish, or a new encoding pipeline.
- (decision) Use explicit progressive-finalization presentation as the default: an early chapter may play but is labelled first-trip-ready/finalizing, hides final-only actions and retains visible render progress until verified full replacement.
- (decision) Require one immutable committed preparation request for a render generation so final assembly cannot lose pre-existing selections, included trips, direction or soundtrack while edit state changes.
- (decision) Fullscreen mute implementation starts with a cross-platform same-player app-owned fullscreen spike; a minimal native fullscreen mute extension is the fallback if the spike cannot preserve playback and listening state.
- (event) Coherence validation PASS: CAP-1 through CAP-4 each have intent and demonstrable success; constraints bend privacy, render ownership, lean UX and platform verification; non-goals exclude a second video/audio product.
- (event) Preservation validation PASS: partial-versus-final truthfulness, exact aggregate composition, audio finalization, fullscreen speaker continuity, progress, privacy and Android/iOS owner evidence are retained in the kernel and three companions. Wrapper-only frustration was omitted.
- (event) Spec finalized for planning: companion paths resolve and whitespace validation passes; no application behavior, build or device state changed by this specification task.
