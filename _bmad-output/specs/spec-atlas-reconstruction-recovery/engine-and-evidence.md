# Engine and evidence contract

This is the follow-up to the Home-first and recognizable reconstruction
contracts. It governs the eight capabilities in this folder. It supersedes
the prior exclusion of visual location inference only for the consented
GPS-less proposal experiment. The earlier optional first-reveal question
and separate Possible trips surface retain their existing semantics.

## CAP-1 — Continuity and independent qualification

Resolve deduplicated, dated evidence into visits and neighboring observation
blocks before qualifying episodes. Evaluate both standalone groups and their
potential join. Time proximity and country transitions are inputs. A time
window alone is insufficient to publish a join, and a border crossing alone
does not require a split. Positive support can include independent intermediate
observations that corroborate continuous travel. Freeze an explicit policy of
sufficient signals and thresholds before holdout scoring. Missing observations
are neither proof of continuity nor proof of a return.

Use effective-dated Home areas and corroborated returns, known time conflicts,
physical plausibility, and protected owner boundaries. A later unrelated Home
observation cannot retroactively close a boundary. A verified owner split,
protected scope, or incompatible evidence suppresses merge suggestions. Owner
decisions do not get rewritten by automatic regrouping.

| Boundary outcome | Atlas result |
| --- | --- |
| Supported continuity and joint qualification | One trip with its ordered, permitted evidence. |
| Plausible but unresolved and both groups independently qualify | Two published trips and one reviewable exact-pair suggestion. |
| Plausible but one group lacks standalone qualification | Retain qualified groups. Use Possible trips only when its existing positive-evidence gate is met. |
| Implausible, conflicting, or protected | Keep independently qualified trips separate and withhold the suggestion. |

CAP-2 proposals are keyed to current versioned support, scope and owner
revision. Confirming the reviewed pair is an explicit correction. Check both
trip identities, permissions, eligibility, current generation, protected facts
and any Replay mapping before committing. Verify the resulting trip and its
exact support before replacing the cards. Treat dismissal as presentation only
unless the user explicitly keeps the groups separate. Retry an uncertain write
against the same command receipt. Never silently remap a saved Replay choice.

## CAP-3 and CAP-6 — Geographic authority

Keep the place search index, selected Home geometry and reverse-label evidence
distinct. Search results carry stable ID, level, locality/parent display name,
coverage version and a point with usable provenance. Use explicit accents and
accent-insensitive matching, including Vietnamese `đ`/`d`. Disambiguate
namesakes. A broad region with only an unverified center can guide a narrower
search. A user can save only a verified point and an explicitly reviewed area
and effective period. Preserve existing saved geometry and dates, including
legacy areas. Do not reinterpret them when the index changes.

Resolve GPS-backed visit support against versioned offline boundary and name
data with recorded provenance, licensing, geographic coverage and valid
periods. Require Vietnamese coverage beyond the reviewed Sa Đéc and Cần Thơ
footprints. Check Huế and additional independent regional controls, near-edge
cases and unaffected countries. Search points may supply names and search
anchors. Boundary geometry supplies geographic membership.

For each stop, prefer its verified owner label or the most specific
corroborated city or province supported by the source and accuracy margin.
When local evidence is ambiguous, use a supported parent if available, then
the country or a neutral fallback. Use a name valid for the observed period
where the source establishes it. Preserve local accents. If the date or
historical coverage cannot support the finer name, use the level that remains
verifiable.

For a multi-stop trip, keep the supported destination labels on the trip
projection. Use a recognizable supported destination summary on the card and
show all verified stops in detail. A stop that resolves only to a country
cannot erase another stop's verified name. Geography changes only naming.
It preserves grouping, Home geometry, owner corrections and Replay selection.

## CAP-4 — Visual location as a proposal

The [automatic visual recovery contract](automatic-visual-recovery.md) governs
candidate discovery, authority, representative selection, recognition,
grouping and publication. Store visual place hypotheses separately from
capture location and from episode membership. Apply the same
current-generation and privacy guards as a local trip correction.
