# Atlas and Home experience

Use Living Atlas surfaces, photo-led trip cards, quiet secondary cues and
accessible native controls. Keep the current navigation, including Settings and
the fixed Replay action. English strings below are working source copy.

## Timeline and merge review

- Sort inferred trips by newest supported observed start, then a stable
  identity tie-break. Undated trips follow dated trips. Explicit owner dates
  remain identifiable as owner claims. Preserve legitimate visit-order and
  date-precision uncertainty. Within-year row order cannot imply precision
  that the evidence lacks.
- Show a supported day or day range where month-only text would make nearby
  cards indistinguishable. Retain honest month/year-only formatting when that
  is the available precision. Avoid repeated status badges and stacked paging
  controls on narrow screens. Keep photos, dates and trip identity recognizable.
- For two visible neighboring cards, use a fine dotted connector with two
  endpoints and one labeled **Review merge suggestion** control. The cue
  indicates a relationship to review and makes no claim about the route.
  When filters, ordering or pagination separate the cards, show a compact
  action that opens the exact pair without drawing a misleading connector
  across unrelated rows. If both trips are outside the view, show no row cue.
- Review presents both recognizable trip cards, their supported periods, the
  bounded evidence for the proposed connection, and a merged preview. Offer
  **Keep separate** and **Confirm merge**. Back leaves both trips as they were.
  After a verified change, update the count, Replay eligibility and selected
  context together. Keep a conflicting or stale pair out of confirmation and
  give a safe return. A failed save must not appear successful.

## Search-first Home

Use the same primary flow in onboarding and Settings. **Search for a place**
accepts local spelling with or without accents. Each result identifies the
locality and its administrative parents. Selecting a usable result centers a
preview with the actual Home area. The owner confirms the effective dates or
chooses all available history, then saves. Retain the existing Home radius
default as a starting choice and display its extent. Coordinate entry and
moving a pin are unnecessary to complete the normal flow.

An ambiguous result needs a selection. A broad or unsupported result can
guide further search, with clear empty or unavailable feedback and Skip where
applicable. Keep text size, touch targets, Back and offline behavior usable.
Adding, editing or skipping Home preserves the current source authority.

## GPS-less recovery hint

Keep Atlas usable while it finds candidate clusters and analyzes a bounded
sample within the authorized scope. The owner enters no dates and selects
no input photos. Surface a quiet **You may be missing a trip** hint when a
reviewable proposal exists. Use the supported trip destination as the title
and show the observed period. A qualified city-level trip appears immediately
in Possible trips, including when only one stop can be named. Choose covers from
different supported days or stops where available. Open the paged album for
the entire qualified membership and show known stop names in detail. Keep
unlocated photos without a capture-place label.

Make **Confirm trip** the primary action and dismissal secondary. Review
shows the exact album membership that confirmation will save. Leave
inconclusive groups unpublished. Repeat hints require materially changed
evidence and a fresh eligibility check. If the earlier proposal was confirmed,
present a correction against the same trip through its existing review
surface after the candidate qualifies.

## Domestic trip titles

Use a verified city or province as the visible destination when available.
For multiple named stops, fit a concise destination summary on the card and
preserve the supported names in detail. Keep observed dates visible to
distinguish trips to the same place. Use the country-only title when the
available evidence supports only that precision.

## Rebuild and album availability

Keep the last committed Timeline and its permitted albums usable while a new
generation builds, including after an interruption where authority still holds.
Show one quiet progress state. Replace the projection only after verified
readback. On a truly empty verified result, explain the available coverage and
offer the existing next action. A stale or unavailable read shows a recoverable
state instead of a successful zero. Revoked evidence is withheld immediately.
Preserve scroll and selected context only when the current generation verifies
the exact authority or a unique safe successor.
