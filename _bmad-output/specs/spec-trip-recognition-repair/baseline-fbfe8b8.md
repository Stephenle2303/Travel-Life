## Reading this table

- **Every "learned Home" abroad trip (S7a, S7b, S9, D, E, G, S13) regresses**
  from `atlas` to `possible` versus its `configured Home` result, confirming
  Finding F4/F5: the current engine's three learned-base definitions give
  weaker qualification than an owner-set Home.
- **Every short domestic trip mismatches even with a configured Home**
  (S3, S5, S10, A, B, C, H): the shadow Possible-trips engine
  (`recognizable-travel-shadow-v1`) picks these up as `possible` while the
  Atlas qualifier (`trip-detector-v1.1`) scores them 50-60 and silently drops
  them to `NOT_TRIP` with an empty reasons list — Finding F1, now measured at
  5 of 50 runs.
- **S18a/S18b expect `hidden` and get `possible`** because the current
  Possible-trips shadow engine has no "single photo" or "not your photo"
  exclusion; S18b additionally exposed Finding F11
  (`qualifyGraphJourneyV11` ignores `sourceOrigin`).
- **S16 expects `possible` and gets `hidden`**: photos with 3-6 km accuracy
  exceed both engines' 1 km reliable-location cutoff and currently produce no
  support at all, so there is nothing to surface as a Possible-trip card.
- **F (configured, isolated, 20 km-apart places) is the one scenario that
  already matches** under both Home modes — it is the only case with enough
  independent place separation to pass `trip-detector-v1.1`'s score-70 gate
  outright.

This baseline is the reference for every M10.3-M10.7 change: a changed
scenario must move toward its label, and `F` and `S1/S2/S4/S6/S14/S19`
(already-matching rows) must not regress.
