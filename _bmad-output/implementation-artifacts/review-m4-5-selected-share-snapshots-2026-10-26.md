## Conclusion

The spec needs revision before treating it as a complete implementation contract. Earlier structural/self-validation and root checks passed, but did not establish behavioral completeness. The earlier unqualified readiness statement is superseded by this review.

The independent behavioral passes overlap on expiry versus immutable content, server transformation after preview, replacement cancellation, lost-response token recovery, delete-all versus pending activation, and storage overwrite races. Their overlapping findings are retained below as independent corroboration, not counted as distinct defects. Additional gaps concern the local privacy/activation boundary, finite deduplication retention, decoder/staging limits, a normative wire schema, selection dependency transitions and quota recovery during replacement. These are specification findings, not observed production failures.
